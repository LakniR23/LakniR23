## 🌟 About Me

I am a passionate **Final-Year (4th-year) Software Engineering undergraduate** at the [Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology (SLIIT)](https://www.sliit.lk/), dedicated to building scalable, resilient, and aesthetically engaging software solutions.

With hands-on experience spanning both modern client-facing applications and robust backend architectures, I bridge the gap between intuitive UI/UX design and enterprise-grade backend engineering:

- 🎨 **Frontend & Mobile Craftsmanship**: Developing pixel-perfect, accessible, and responsive user interfaces with **React.js, Next.js, Angular, React Native, and Tailwind CSS**.
- ⚙️ **Backend & Microservices**: Architecting high-throughput RESTful services and modular systems with **Spring Boot, Spring Modulith, Node.js, and Express.js**.
- 🗄️ **Data Architecture & Serverless**: Designing optimized schemas for **PostgreSQL, MySQL, and MongoDB**, alongside serverless data management using **Supabase** and **Neon DB**.
- ☁️ **DevOps & Cloud-Native**: Containerizing microservices with **Docker**, managing container workloads with **Kubernetes**, and implementing automated CI/CD deployments.
- 🤖 **AI Integrations**: Embedding intelligent conversational assistants and diagnostic recommendation engines powered by the **Gemini API** and **Groq LLaMA**.
